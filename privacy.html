Privacy policy

ile-youtube asks for one permission: uploading videos to the YouTube channel you choose (scope youtube.upload).

The access token stays on the developer's own computer. It is never shared, sold or sent anywhere except to Google's YouTube API.

ile-youtube does not read, collect or store any other data from your Google account.

You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact: open an issue at github.com/SkymaaX/skymaax.github.io.
